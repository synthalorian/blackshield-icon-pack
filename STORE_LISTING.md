## Full description (4000 chars max)
Blackshield Icons is a full-coverage icon pack with a single, uncompromising
language: a dark steel tile, a bone-white glyph, and one blood-red accent.
No rainbow chaos. No half-themed home screen.

**48,000+ app components mapped — 13,700+ apps — 100% coverage of the
Arcticons database.** If an app exists, Blackshield themes it. And for the
apps nobody's ever heard of, Blackshield wraps them automatically: unthemed
icons are masked, scaled, and set on the same steel tile, so your home screen
never breaks formation.

**The look**
- Dark steel tile with a subtle vertical gradient and hairline edge
- Bone-gradient glyphs, redrawn on a consistent 24-grid
- A single blood accent — one dash, every icon, no exceptions
- Adaptive icons, monochrome (themed icons) support, and 20 launchers
  supported out of the box — including Nova, Lawnchair, Smart Launcher,
  Niagara, Action, and more

**Also available: Blackshield Red** — the canon swap. Blood glyphs on steel
with a bone dash, for when the grid runs hot.

No ads. No tracking. No internet permission. Just icons.
